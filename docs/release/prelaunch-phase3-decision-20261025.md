## Final legal, privacy and security findings

1. **Rights and editorial integrity — HOLD.** Q-306-01..16 include 14 OPEN and two BLOCKED decisions. None has the required positive item-specific public-rights, lawful processing, reviewer, attribution and material-change receipt. Published literal source words, quotations, attachments and AI-generated claims need source+version+use evidence; a public link and CC BY alone do not settle personal-data necessity, third-party copyrights or reputational claims. Maintain original/translation separation, deny person-level scores or intent inferences, and quarantine minors and sensitive data. See [GDPR Arts. 5, 6, 9, 17, 85](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [Italian copyright Arts. 65/70](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1941-04-22;633), [the Garante journalistic-data decision 759 (23 September 2026)](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/10307852), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
2. **Privacy — HOLD.** `PUBLIC_STATEMENTS`, `SOURCE_COPIES`, `PERSON_IDENTITIES`, `RIGHTS_REQUESTS`, `OPERATIONAL_LOGS`, `BACKUPS` are all BLOCKED in `docs/policy/privacy-processing-decisions-v1.json`. `OPTIONAL_ACCOUNTS` is EXCLUDED. Named controller/processor responsibility, lawful basis and exception analysis, purpose-limited fields, real notices, access and retention/erasure schedules for logs, cookies, SQLite WAL, snapshots and off-host backups lack operational acceptance. Nothing in an inventory of fields constitutes processing approval. Use GDPR Arts. 5, 6, 13–17, 25, 32–35 and the relevant Italian Code provisions.
3. **Corrections, reply and revocation — HOLD.** API cache race hardening from [PR #387](https://github.com/domenicomassafra/DichiarazioniPubbliche.it/pull/387) is on `main`, but there is no authenticated public item to revoke and no witnessed notice → review → publication invalidation → API/static/CDN readback → rollback on the same candidate. Keep private, append-only challenge/correction state, and keep the affected source or item out of public projection. [#98](https://github.com/domenicomassafra/DichiarazioniPubbliche.it/issues/98) owns the full rehearsal. A technical empty-page readback cannot prove this workflow.
