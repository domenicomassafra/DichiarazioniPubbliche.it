## What is decided

1. **One integrated reader experience.** Exact attributable public wording and source/context inspection, factual checks including genuinely insufficient evidence, comparisons across people and across a person's history, and Person/Topic/Content/Trace navigation all belong to the product. Opinions and expressed positions may be documented without a factual Finding. Pilot scope tests this complete mission; it is not an archive-only pivot.
2. **Verbatim is the declaration.** No normalized text, paraphrase, translation or summary may impersonate the speaker's original exact words, including cards, headings, search, excerpts, API, machine-readable output or comparison views. An explicitly marked synopsis and separately identified Italian translation can supplement an original, not substitute for it. Fidelity needs binding to source version, literal range(s), speaker, provenance and sufficient meaning-preserving context; ASR/OCR and edited/discontinuous passages require special tests.
3. **Rights and fidelity are distinct.** A source being reachable, official or old does not clear capture, transcription, quotation, translation, image, recording or republication. Determine a concrete source-family lawful path; where exact wording cannot lawfully be shown, hold that public declaration and investigate alternatives rather than publishing a paraphrase as the declaration. Qualified legal authority and Q-306 dispositions remain external decisions.
4. **Speech acts govern attribution.** Reported historical slogans, irony, dramatization, interviewer prompts, denials, endorsement, criticism and qualifications must not merge into a Person's own stance. A prior utterance, the meaning of that utterance, a later denial of having *said* versus *supported* something, the historical verification of that denial and truth of the underlying proposition are separate questions. Similar words or topic overlap alone do not prove same-take or change of position; divergence is not falsehood or intent to mislead.
5. **Automation target with evidence gates.** Routine human textual review is unavailable. The future design must support bounded active investigation, autonomous checked factual outcomes and eventually per-result publication after rigorous acceptance, including procedural/judicial material. An official document can prove what it records but not automatically an allegation's truth. The present DP-309/310/311 reviewer and publication gates remain in force until an explicit safe versioned replacement passes independent evaluation, concurrency/revocation checks, privacy/rights rules and the necessary owner/legal authority. Multiple agents do not constitute independent sources or human reviewers.
6. **Permanent multilingual, multi-persona corpus.** Original-language words and explicit translations, provenance, semantic assurance (including German), versioned coverage, dedup and batch isolation are part of the target. Hitler/Mussolini are proposed pilot subjects, not fabricated or pre-authorized corpora. A person being deceased does not imply all media rights expire or all documentary versions are fixed. No bulk collection precedes the rights-gated complete pilot acceptance.
7. **Persona × Tema is a filter intersection.** Dated, source-cited, precomputed and versioned summaries of documented expressed positions can accompany exact declarations. No public chatbot and no per-request LLM are required. Summaries must disclose coverage/uncertainty, avoid ungrounded perpetual mental-state claims, and update for corrections or source versions.
8. **Operations must be measured.** The approximate EUR10/month target is an empirical hypothesis, not a current bill or API entitlement. GPT-6 via existing Codex/ChatGPT-web access is a preference, not a license to invent quotas. Groq ASR, provider estimates versus billed spend, included-account usage, unknown quota and local incremental electricity are distinct. Future workers need an incremental cheap default, bounded source refresh, observability, caps, outage/backoff and receipts, but this planning run does not execute provider or production tests.
9. **Owner assets and future UI choice.** Zero ordinary textual reviewers; the owner may contribute approximately five hours/week of primarily visual checks. An approved image is not approval of a statement/verdict. Redesign will be selected by the owner only after side-by-side alternatives on the same genuine or clearly labelled fixture state. Google reader login/registration/logout/deletion remains in future scope; private by default and not Studio/editorial authority. Public intake/admin activation remains separately gated.
