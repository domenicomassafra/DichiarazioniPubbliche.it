# DP-400 — Competitive UX research and dual-surface design brief

Status: DONE  
Milestone: M4 research track  
Depends on: M0

## Problem

Dichiarazioni Pubbliche risks being perceived as legal/institutional software if the name and public
record architecture dominate the presentation. At the same time, the planned private
live verification workflow has very different information-density needs from the public
site.

## Outcome

Study international fact-checking/editorial products and live verification tools, extract
reusable UX patterns, define Public vs Verify Studio as distinct surfaces under one brand
system, and prepare controlled visual directions for mockup generation.

## Acceptance criteria

- research includes Italian and international public fact-checkers;
- research includes live/professional verification tools;
- explicit adopt / do-not-copy patterns;
- public and private/operator IA are separated;
- design principles reject legalistic and gamified truth-score aesthetics;
- at least four materially different concept prompts exist;
- a fixed evaluation rubric exists before mockups are generated.

Research set includes Pagella Politica, Facta, Newtral, Full Fact, Factiverse,
PolitiFact, Snopes, AFP Fact Check, Maldita, Africa Check, Google Fact Check Explorer and
