# DP-306/307 — Italian and EU primary-source research, 20 October 2026

Status: dated **conservative product dispositions** under the owner's 20 October 2026 direction in [issue #55](https://github.com/domenicomassafra/DichiarazioniPubbliche.it/issues/55#issuecomment-6287288216). Jurisdiction: Italy, public web site accessible in the EU; editorial publication of sourced public statements, with ingestion and review kept private. No public user-generated submissions or independent AI chat service assumed.

The owner removed the requirement to commission outside counsel. This document records substantive answers and cautious product choices; it is **not** a professional legal opinion, a finding that every exception applies, a source-specific licence, or a production-launch authorization. The original DP-306 launch register and the independent publication gates remain authoritative. Each entry separates **Rule** (what the primary source says) from **Application** (our interpretation and accepted conservative engineering policy), then names **Condition** and **Residual risk**. A source link establishes its instrument, never a licence for a particular work or permission to publish a particular person.

## Primary sources verified for this decision packet

- **R1** — [Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), 27 April 2016, applicable from 25 May 2018: Arts. 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12–22, 25, 30, 32–35, 37, 85, 89; the existing DP-306 register contains earlier text receipts.
- **R2** — [Italian Privacy Code, Legislative Decree 196/2003, coordinated Garante text](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/documents/10160/0/Codice%2Bin%2Bmateria%2Bdi%2Bprotezione%2Bdei%2Bdati%2Bpersonali%2B%28Testo%2Bcoordinato%29), 30 June 2003, as amended by Legislative Decree 101/2018 and subsequent amendments: Arts. 2-quater, 122, **136–139**. The substantive text of Arts. 136–139 was read in the Garante's coordinated text (pp. 57–58). Article 136(c) expressly covers some occasional publications or expressions, but the purposes/conditions require assessment.
- **R3** — [Garante resolution 491, 29 November 2018, rules on journalistic processing](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/web/guest/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/9067692), published in G.U. 4 January 2019 no. 3: Annex A.1 Arts. 1, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12. Applicable safeguards concerning essentiality, dignity and minors; not a blanket exemption.
- **R4** — [ECtHR Grand Chamber, Axel Springer AG v Germany](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng?i=001-109034), app. 39954/08, judgment **7 February 2012**, especially §§ 89–95; case-specific Article 8/10 balance, public interest, notoriety, acquisition/veracity, content and consequences. Persuasive criteria, not a universal safe harbour.
- **R5** — [Law 47/1948 on the press, Art. 8](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1948-02-08;47~art8), 8 February 1948 (as subsequently amended): right of reply/rectification of affected persons for covered daily/periodical/news-agency publications. Its precise applicability to this site's legal classification requires a deployment-specific determination.
- **R6** — [Law 633/1941 on copyright](https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:1941-04-22;633), 22 April 1941 (consolidated amendments through 2024): Arts. 1, 13, 15, 65, 70 and database protections. Art. 70 allows excerpts for criticism/discussion within justified limits, non-substitution, and required attribution; **it sets no universal word count**.
- **R7** — [Directive 2001/29/EC (InfoSoc)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2001/29/oj), 22 May 2001: Art. 5(3)(c) for current-event reporting and 5(3)(d) for quotation/criticism/review, subject to conditions and national implementation. Art. 5(3)(a) covers teaching/scientific illustration; it is **not** the general quotation exception.
- **R8** — [Directive (EU) 2019/790 (CDSM)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/790/oj), 17 April 2019: Arts. 3–4 concern text and data mining, subject to specific limitations and reservations of rights, **not** publishing excerpts.
- **R9** — [Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (DSA)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj), 19 October 2022, generally applicable from 17 February 2024: Arts. 3(g),(h),(i), 6, 8, 11–17, 20. Hosting and online-platform obligations depend on the actual service, not on calling it editorial.
- **R10** — [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act), consolidated 27 July 2026](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727), original 13 June 2024: Arts. 3, 4, 50, 113. Art. 4 literacy duties and Art. 50's role- and output-dependent transparency duties must be assessed separately.
- **R11** — [Garante cookie and tracking guidance, resolution 231](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/9677876), 10 June 2021, G.U. 9 July 2021 no. 163: Italian Privacy Code Art. 122; GDPR Arts. 7, 12, 13, 25.

These are statutory/regulatory texts, an authority's published rules and a court judgment. Platform terms, individual licences, service logs and deployed software are **not** established by these sources. Dates above identify the legal act or judgment; research date is 20 October 2026. Recheck consolidated law and contracts on any later release.

## Sixteen dispositions (one-to-one with DP-306)

### Q-306-01 — factual findings and editorial status
**Rule:** R1 Art. 85 permits Member State reconciliation of privacy and expression; R2 Arts. 136(c), 137(3) and R3 Arts. 5–6 require a public-interest/essentiality assessment even for some occasional publication; R4 §§ 89–95 describes the competing-rights factors. **Application:** a reasoned, source-bounded finding about a *proposition*, never a blanket verdict on a person, is the cautious editorial form. **Condition:** manual attribution, contemporaneous evidence, relevance, source and correction route, dated independent editorial review, neutral context in all public channels. **Residual risk:** defamation, harm from false attribution, disputed classification under Art. 136 and inaccurate data. Hold real findings pending case-level review.

### Q-306-02 — intentionality and deception
**Rule:** Neither R1 Art. 85 nor R2 Art. 137 makes a contradiction proof of knowledge, motive or intent; R4 requires proportionality and veracity assessment. **Application:** prohibit any public intent/deception/lie classification or person scores, including in metadata and translations. **Condition:** retain the existing closed claim-level vocabulary and denylist; changing it requires a new independent decision. **Residual risk:** contextual phrasing can still imply dishonesty; require human copy review.

### Q-306-03 — right-of-reply intake
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 5–7, 12–14, 16–17 and 21 govern a separately collected submission, transparency, lawful basis, rights and, where applicable, withdrawal; R5 Art. 8 governs qualifying press rectifications. **Application:** keep untrusted public intake disabled until a dedicated privacy notice, basis per field, authenticated or proportionately verified channel, processor/access register and tested rights workflow exist. **Condition:** private, least-data request routing and receipt without automatic exposure; do not represent withdrawal as deleting lawful editorial history. **Residual risk:** impersonation and sensitive unsolicited content.

### Q-306-04 — reply hosting / DSA classification
**Rule:** R9 Art. 3(g),(h),(i) distinguishes mere conduit, caching, hosting and online platforms; Arts. 16–17 add notice/action and reasons for hosting providers. **Application:** an editor-controlled record without third-party public publication is *provisionally* treated as editorial; no claim of legal exemption. **Condition:** intake and community posting remain disabled; if third-party stored content is made public at users' request, reassess DSA classification and implement applicable mechanisms before activation. **Residual risk:** even private reply storage could qualify as hosting on actual facts.

### Q-306-05 — logs, cookies, anti-abuse
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 5(1)(c),(e),(f), 6, 25, 32 set minimization, storage and security duties; R2 Art. 122 and R11 require consent for nonessential terminal tracking and relevant notice. **Application:** no fingerprinting, marketing/analytics identifiers, persistent visitor buckets or optional account cookies without an approved purpose and consent/basis as applicable. **Condition:** inventory exact HTTP log fields, access, retention/deletion and technical cookies before enabling, redact credentials/IP where feasible, document any necessity. **Residual risk:** CDN, reverse proxy and backup logs may retain identifiers outside application controls.

### Q-306-06 — correction, retraction, reply
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 16, 17, 18, 21 create qualified rights subject to exceptions; R5 Art. 8 sets specific duties for qualifying press publications; R4 requires balancing expression and reputation. **Application:** operate a private, documented notice/review channel with prompt triage, correction or restricted visibility where necessary; preserve explanatory version history when lawful. **Condition:** distinguish factual error, changed fact, disputed opinion, privacy or rights complaint; do not promise a universal statutory time limit to every claimant. **Residual risk:** actual classification may trigger specific Art. 8 periods or judicial urgency.

### Q-306-07 — review authority and appeals
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 5(2), 12, 16–18, 24 require accountable handling where applicable; R9 Art. 17 may require statements of reasons for a hosting service. **Application:** make editor, challenger and deciding reviewer roles distinct where possible, record conflicts and case-level reason; do not let an LLM decide takedown. **Condition:** access-controlled evidence, appeal path, reversible public suppression, dated audit and documented exceptional single-reviewer decision. **Residual risk:** small-team reviewer independence and timeliness are limited.

### Q-306-08 — controller, legal basis, people and accounts
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 4(7),(8), 5–6, 9, 13–15, 21, 26–28, 30, 85; R2 Arts. 136–139 provide specific expression rules that depend on purpose and essentiality. **Application:** treat DP as responsible controller for its own editorial and operational purposes; record a *separate* lawful basis for each data class (public statements, source copies, identity, optional account, requests, logs). The journalistic path cannot be assumed for accounts. **Condition:** minimization and notices by purpose, processor map/contract, rights contact and provenance before any personal public projection; OIDC member accounts remain off until assessed. **Residual risk:** legal-basis and joint-controller determinations can vary by feed/provider/hosting.

### Q-306-09 — sensitive data, minors and allegations
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 9–10 restrict special-category and criminal-offence data; R2 Art. 137(1),(3) conditions journalistic derogations on R3 and public-interest essentiality; R3 Arts. 5–10 demand heightened dignity and minors safeguards. **Application:** default quarantine and no trait inference, minors/victims/private addresses/criminal allegations hidden unless documented exceptional public necessity. **Condition:** human review per record, source/public-role relevance, prefer generalized/redacted identifiers and fresh criminal-case status. **Residual risk:** a technically accurate quote may still disproportionately expose protected information.

### Q-306-10 — retention, rights, incidents
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 5(1)(e), 17–18, 30, 32–34 require purpose-limited storage, qualified erasure/restriction and incident handling; Art. 33 generally requires supervisory notification within 72 hours of awareness unless breach unlikely to risk rights/freedoms. **Application:** no universal retention period can be derived from statute; existing retention approval stays off. **Condition:** inventory copies, WAL, export, caches, audit logs and backups by purpose; set dated schedules, holds, restriction propagation and breach-response ownership before personal intake/account launch. **Residual risk:** deletion in immutable archives/backups, conflicting legal holds and late discovery.

### Q-306-11 — quotation, exact text and excerpts
**Rule:** R6 Arts. 65, 70 and R7 Art. 5(3)(c),(d) allow *bounded* exceptions subject to national conditions; R8 Arts. 3–4 concern mining, not public redistribution. **Application:** exact words are a product requirement but confer no right to republish. **Condition:** require a source-family/content-version rights receipt, purpose-specific public use, attribution, proportionate amount, no substitute publication and up-to-date source hash; prefer owner/licensor permission when unclear. No global safe character/word threshold. **Residual risk:** source-specific neighbouring, broadcast, database and moral rights or contractual limits.

### Q-306-12 — source terms, scraping and API
**Rule:** R6 covers copyright and databases; R8 Arts. 3–4 place conditions on TDM and rights reservations; lawful technical access does not itself confer redistribution rights. **Application:** allowlist by original source, account, terms, licence, endpoint and use case. **Condition:** examine each actual platform's current written terms, robots/access restrictions and rate rules; pause unresolved source adapters and do not bypass auth, paywalls or technical measures. **Residual risk:** terms change, access rights differ from quotation rights, database extraction may be restricted.

### Q-306-13 — machine transcripts and public disclosures
**Rule:** R10 Art. 50(2) addresses synthetic-output marking by *providers*; Art. 50(4) addresses certain deepfakes and public-interest AI-generated text by *deployers*, with specific editorial-review qualification. It does not declare ordinary ASR transcripts automatically authoritative. **Application:** identify ASR method and human corrections distinctly from authenticated original words; never call an unreviewed ASR output a literal approved quotation. **Condition:** preserve source and model provenance, label unverified/generated material and escalate any synthetic manipulation to a distinct Art. 50 assessment. **Residual risk:** product/provider role boundaries and imperfect accuracy/marking.

### Q-306-14 — cross-cutting AI, DSA, ePrivacy
**Rule:** R9 Art. 3 uses functional service definitions; R10 Arts. 3, 4, 50 attach duties to actual AI roles and outputs; R2 Art. 122/R11 govern terminal tracking. **Application:** assess each feature separately instead of claiming a global editorial exception. **Condition:** static approved read pages only under baseline; before accounts, reply storage, embeds, AI public interaction or new tracker, produce a deployment-role matrix and confirm notices, contacts, transparency and moderation obligations. **Residual risk:** real deployment behavior may differ from diagrams or evolve with third parties.

### Q-306-15 — terms, privacy, contact, governance
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 12–14, 24, 32–34 require meaningful information, responsibility and incident controls; R9 Arts. 11–14 and 16 may apply to intermediary services; R5 Art. 8 may apply to a qualifying publication. **Application:** publish no production personal-record surface without owned contact and working complaint/rights route, plain-language privacy notice, security incident owner and available terms applicable to real features. **Condition:** verify deliverability, test receipt and handling, track third-party providers and signed operational responsibility before production acceptance. **Residual risk:** website legal designation, registration/insurance and sector obligations are fact-dependent and unverified; conservatively avoid claiming exemption.

### Q-306-16 — stale decision / mandatory re-review
**Rule:** R1 Arts. 5(2), 24–25, 35 are ongoing accountability/risk-control duties; R9 and R10 role applicability changes with actual functionality. **Application:** every earlier disposition is conditional, time- and scope-bounded and automatically loses product approval when the material premise changes. **Condition:** reopen the affected Q on new jurisdiction, product audience, source family/contract, data class, model/AI role, publication wording, provider, hosting, public UGC, incident or adverse decision; make each case reviewer record date + new evidence + test receipt. Next review: before first real publication and at each material change. **Residual risk:** undetected changes and future legal interpretation.

## Implementation and release boundary

The 16 answers are **accepted conservative design instructions** under the 20 October owner delegation; they are *not* 16 unconditional findings of substantive legal compliance. No new permission to publish content, transmit private data, open intake, activate accounts or release AI output is granted. The current launch register continues to mark legal/public-surface closure as OPEN or BLOCKED; its release preflight remains NO-GO until each planned public use is independently justified by an evidence/rights/operational receipt and the actual release acceptance. The owner can choose to accept the residual risk of a specific launch only on that separately documented, dated record. Original exact wording remains withheld until a content/source-specific clearance and reviewed projection exist.
